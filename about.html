Our Story

Who We Are

Comfort Transport Services (Pty) Ltd is more than just a transport company — we are a team committed to trust, reliability, and dignity on every journey. We understand that for many of our passengers, transport is not a luxury; it's a lifeline.

We specialise in patient transport, with a particular focus on dialysis patients who depend on punctual, safe, and compassionate service for their treatment appointments. We take that responsibility seriously, every single day.

Based in Polokwane, we proudly serve the surrounding areas and operate routes across Limpopo, Gauteng, and all provinces nationwide. Whether it's a hospital appointment, airport transfer, or a long-distance shuttle, you're in safe hands with us.

Polokwane Observer

Founder Khuthadzo Rametsi featured in the Polokwane Observer — photographed with the vehicle he uses to transport patients.

By Ronewa Phaladi — Polokwane Observer

Patient-focused transport service to access healthcare facilities

Local businessman Khuthadzo Rametsi (27) is making a difference in the lives of healthcare patients through Comfort Transport Services, which provides reliable transport for individuals and businesses.

Rametsi founded the company in 2024 after identifying a gap in the market for safe and dependable transport, particularly for people who rely on mobility services to access healthcare facilities.

The business currently works with Bophelong Renal Care Dialysis and transports patients to Pietersburg Provincial Hospital, ensuring they reach their medical appointments on time and safely.

It's not just about moving people from one place to another. It's about reliability, dignity, and making sure people reach the places that matter most in their lives.

— Khuthadzo Rametsi, Founder

His goal is to grow Comfort Transport Services into a trusted transport solution across Limpopo and build long-term relationships with hospitals, businesses and individuals — while continuing to improve the quality of service.
