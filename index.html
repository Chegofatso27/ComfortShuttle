Frequently Asked Questions

Is the deposit refundable?
No, the deposit is non-refundable. It secures your spot and the balance can be paid as per the package terms.
Do you accommodate large groups?
Yes, we cater to all group sizes. Contact us for group rates and special arrangements.
How far in advance should I book?
We recommend booking early, especially during peak seasons. We're available 24/7 and will do our best to accommodate urgent requests.
What happens if my trip is cancelled?
If we cancel due to unforeseen issues, you'll receive a full refund or the option to reschedule. For personal cancellations, please refer to our cancellation policy.
Do you offer wheelchair-accessible vehicles?
We accommodate patients with mobility needs. Please mention any special requirements when booking so we can ensure the right vehicle is arranged for you.
What areas do you serve?
We are based in Polokwane and serve the surrounding areas, as well as Gauteng and all other provinces across South Africa.
